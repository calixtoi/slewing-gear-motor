Executive Summary

This document defines the slewing drive motor dimensioning for two PF crane variants: PM010 and PM401. Following the existing PF200 design philosophy, the motor is sized to cover a minimum 30% share of the worst-case structural peak torque — not the full peak. Two operating scenarios are evaluated: Transport Position operation (maximum 43 kNm) and Substation operation (maximum 62 kNm). The Substation scenario was selected as the worst-case dimensioning basis due to its higher structural torque.
