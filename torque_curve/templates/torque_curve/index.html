How to read this chart

X axis — Motor speed as a percentage of rated speed (0% = motor just started, 100% = normal running speed)

Y axis — Torque at the gearbox output shaft in Nm. This is the torque entering the slewing ring worm.

Red dashed line — Structural limit at the gearbox output shaft (= crane structural limit ÷ CF). If the blue curve starts ABOVE this line, the slewing ring is overloaded at every motor startup.

PointSpeedMeaning
● Locked rotor0%Torque at the instant the motor starts. Checked against structural limit.
● Pull-up~40%Minimum torque during acceleration. Motor must overcome this to reach rated speed.
● Breakdown (Mk)~80%Peak torque. Motor stalls if load exceeds this.
● Nominal100%Rated operating torque. Normal crane slewing.
